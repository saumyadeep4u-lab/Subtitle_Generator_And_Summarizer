This lecture covers supervised machine learning paradigms, decomposing generalization error into bias, variance, and irreducible noise. It contrasts linear models and support vector machines with ensemble methods including Random Forests and Gradient Boosted Decision Trees. Strict train, validation, and held-out test partitioning prevents data leakage during hyperparameter tuning. Finally, precision, recall, F1 score, and ROC-AUC provide calibrated evaluation under severe class imbalance.
